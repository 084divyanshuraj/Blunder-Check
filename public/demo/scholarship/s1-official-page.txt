Aurora Foundation: Aurora Merit Scholarship 2026-27
Last updated: 12 August 2026

About the scholarship
The Aurora Merit Scholarship supports undergraduate students from low-income families who have performed well in school. The award is Rs. 50,000 per academic year, paid directly to the student's bank account.

Eligibility
Applicants must have scored at least 85% in Class XII. The annual family income must be below Rs. 6,00,000. Applicants must be 22 years of age or younger on 1 September 2026.

Documents required
Applicants must upload the following documents: income certificate issued by the Tehsildar, Class XII marksheet, a copy of the bank passbook, and one passport-size photograph.

Important dates
The last date to submit applications is 15 November 2026. Shortlisted candidates will be notified by email.

How to apply
Applications are accepted only through the online portal linked on this page. Offline applications will not be considered.
