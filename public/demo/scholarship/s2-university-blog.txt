Scholarships to watch this season: Aurora Merit Scholarship

Here is what you need to know if you plan to apply for the Aurora Merit Scholarship this year.

Documents to keep ready: income certificate or ITR acknowledgement, Class XII marksheet, bank passbook, and a caste certificate if applicable.

Deadline: Our office has noted that applications close on 30 November 2026, so there is still plenty of time.

The award is Rs. 50,000 per year. The scholarship may be renewed in later years, but students should check with the foundation for the renewal conditions.

Students who need help with the application can visit the Student Affairs office on the second floor.
