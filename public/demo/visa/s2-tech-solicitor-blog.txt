UK Tech Nation Global Talent Visa Overview & Fast-Track Route
Updated: 20 February 2026

Fast-track endorsement applications are processed within 3 weeks under the qualifying incubator and accelerator route. If your startup was backed by an authorized accelerator, you qualify for priority turnaround.

You only need 2 letters of recommendation if you are an exceptional promise applicant who graduated from an authorized incubator program within the past 12 months.

Total official filing fees are £716.

Applicants can submit up to 10 pieces of documentary evidence demonstrating their contribution to the digital technology ecosystem. Letters from mentors or early investors are strongly encouraged.
