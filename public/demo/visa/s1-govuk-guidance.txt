UK Visas and Immigration: Global Talent Visa (Digital Technology)
Policy Guidance published: 15 January 2026

To apply for Stage 1 (Endorsement) under Digital Technology, applicants must provide exactly 3 letters of recommendation from recognized leaders in the digital technology sector. Each letter must be dated within 3 months of application and must be written on official letterhead.

The standard processing time for the endorsement stage is typically up to 8 weeks.

The endorsement application fee is £524 (Stage 1) and the visa application fee is £192 (Stage 2). In addition, applicants must pay the Immigration Health Surcharge (£1,035 per year of visa requested).

Mandatory Criterion: Applicants must demonstrate that they have been recognized as a leading talent or potential talent in digital technology.
Optional Criteria: Applicants must demonstrate at least 2 of the 4 optional criteria (innovation, recognition beyond routine work, significant technical/commercial contribution, or academic research).

All applications must be submitted via the official Home Office digital portal.
