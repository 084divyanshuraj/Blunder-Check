Northfield Institute: Hostel Allotment 2026-27
Last updated: 20 July 2026

Hostel fee for the academic year 2026-27 is Rs. 68,000, payable in two instalments. The first instalment is due at the time of allotment and the second is due by 15 January 2027.

Eligibility: First-year students admitted through the main counselling round are eligible. Allotment is made on a first-come, first-served basis after fee payment.

Required documents: admission letter, two passport-size photographs, and a parent or guardian declaration form.

Hostel gates close at 9:30 PM on weekdays.

For hostel queries, contact the Chief Warden's office.
