Northfield Students Forum: "Hostel FAQ for freshers"
Posted: 2 August 2026

Hey everyone! Quick summary of what seniors told me. The hostel fee is Rs. 72,000 for the year including mess charges. Rooms are shared by three students. You need your admission letter and a medical fitness certificate on the day of allotment. Gates close around 10 PM, but nobody really checks.

Pro tip: Pay the fee early, otherwise you may lose your room.
